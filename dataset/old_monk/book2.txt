The ancient kingdom of Eldoria was ruled by King Arthur. 
The king lived in the Crystal Palace with his trusted advisor Merlin.

In 1205, a great battle occurred between Eldoria and the neighboring kingdom of Norland. 
Many soldiers fought bravely to protect their homeland.

After the battle, King Arthur signed a peace agreement with Norland. 
The people of Eldoria celebrated the victory and hoped for many years of peace.

Merlin recorded the events in the royal history book stored inside the palace library.
