Dr. William Carter was a famous historian from Oxford University. 
He studied ancient civilizations and discovered many historical documents.

In March 2020, Dr. Carter traveled to Rome to research the Roman Empire. 
He visited the Colosseum and collected information about Emperor Augustus.

During his research, he wrote a book called "The Forgotten Empires". 
The book explained the rise and fall of several ancient kingdoms.

Students around the world read his work and learned about history and culture.
