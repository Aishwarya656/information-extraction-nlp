The Old Monk lived near the Greenwood Forest in a small village called Riverdale. 
Many people visited the monk because he was known for his wisdom and kindness.

One morning, a young man named John traveled to the village to meet the Old Monk. 
John asked the monk for guidance about his future. The monk advised John to work hard and remain patient.

After the meeting, John returned to his home in London. He remembered the lessons given by the monk and followed them throughout his life.

The village celebrated the Old Monk every year on 15 August because of his contribution to the community.
